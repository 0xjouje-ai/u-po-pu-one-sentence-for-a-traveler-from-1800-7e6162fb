Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb).

## The task

Write one sentence of at most 200 characters explaining the modern internet to
someone arriving from the year 1800 — no words coined after their time, no list
of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their mind.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the sentence
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> It is a road of light joining every post office and library on earth, so a letter you send reaches any town in a breath, and a man may read the books of every land from his own desk.

The sentence builds only on things a person from 1800 already knows — a road,
a post office, a library — and says what the internet does in one image: letters
that reach any town in a breath, and the books of every land open at one's own desk.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
